## 🚀 Quick Start Demo (2-3 minutes)

### Prerequisites ✅
- Backend running on http://localhost:3000 ✅
- Frontend running on http://localhost:8080 ✅
- Both servers started successfully ✅

### Demo Steps

#### **Step 1: Business Onboarding (30 seconds)**
1. Open http://localhost:8080 in browser
2. Fill in business details:
   - **Name**: "Florăria Magica"
   - **Description**: "Florărie locală din Chișinău cu aranjamente unice pentru nunți și evenimente speciale"
   - **Target Customers**: "Mirese, organizatori de evenimente, persoane care vor cadouri speciale"
   - **Goals**: "Să atrag mai mulți clienți pentru evenimente mari, să cresc vizibilitatea online"
3. Click "🪄 Generează-mi Planul de Marketing"

#### **Step 2: Persona Review & Image Upload (45 seconds)**
1. **Review AI-generated personas**:
   - Maria Popescu (28-35, interested in special events)
   - Ana Ciobanu (30-45, event organizer)

2. **Upload test images** (optional):
   - Drag & drop flower arrangement photos
   - Or skip to let AI generate all images

3. **Enable image generation**: ✅ Generate missing images
4. Click "✅ Confirmă și Continuă"

#### **Step 3: Enhanced Campaign Generation (45 seconds)**
**Watch the magic happen:**
- ✨ AI creates targeted social media posts
- 🎨 DALL-E generates professional marketing images
- 🔍 Smart algorithm matches uploaded images to posts
- 📱 Content optimized for Facebook/Instagram
- 🚀 Auto-posting to Facebook enabled

**Results shown:**
- 3-6 posts with Romanian text and hashtags
- Image processing summary
- Auto-posting status
- Planable workspace created

#### **Step 4: Campaign Results (30 seconds)**
**Dashboard displays:**
- 📈 Marketing strategy overview

- 🎯 Auto-posting confirmation
